01 · Understand the space

Start with your building

Tell the contractor whether you are improving an attic, crawl space, occupied room or another assembly. Include access, moisture concerns and the stage of your renovation.

02 · Prepare the brief

Make the first conversation useful

Have your city, photos, approximate dimensions and desired timing ready. Describe the comfort, noise or renovation issue you want to address.

03 · Confirm the approach

Get a project-specific assessment

Ask about suitable materials, preparation, access, ventilation and the scope of the quote. Product selection and installation details require professional review.
